What changedThe web platform's visual language: its type, color, cards and glass. Flows and navigation are untouched, so nothing has to be relearned.
What is newOpportunity imagery and filters, the Ascent milestone view, and a Home that leads with asking a mentor. All three already proven on the web platform.

What comes nextBeta feedback, then it replaces the app in the App Store.

About the captures. Every pair is a real screenshot of both builds on an iPhone 17 Pro Max at the default text size, showing the same account and the same data, so every difference on this page is design rather than content. The mentee pairs were captured against production: the only people who appear are mentors, who are listed on the public registry. The mentor pairs were captured against staging with test accounts, so no real student appears anywhere on this page.
